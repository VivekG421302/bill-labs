Bulk create from CSV

Each CSV row becomes a bill made from the rows above. A column whose header matches a Key (for example Bill No, Vehi.No, Date) fills that row's value. An Amount column also works out Preset, Sale and Volume from the rate. Empty cells keep the value above.

Editing rows

  • Swipe right on a row to open the blue panel, then tap its button to switch the row between Key Value and Full Line.
  • Swipe left to open the red delete button.
  • Drag the dots to move a row up or down.
  • Tap the eye to switch to the preview, hold it to peek.
  • Install: Chrome menu, Install app (Add to Home screen). Safari: Share, Add to Home Screen.
